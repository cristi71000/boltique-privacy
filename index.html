Boltique privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Boltique ("the game") is made by Cristian Donciulescu (Chitila, Romania), "we" below. This policy says what the game collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob and measures play with GameAnalytics. Both receive device and usage data described below.
  • Personalised ads and tracking happen only if you allow them. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy options.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, sharing data with ad partners for personalised ads counts as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out of it as below.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. Change it any time: Settings, then Privacy options.
  • US state privacy rights: to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for personalised ads, open Settings, then Privacy options.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks before it may track you (App Tracking Transparency). You can change it in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • Fewer ads: the Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between levels; rewarded ads stay optional.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. We hold no account data ourselves; for data held by Google and GameAnalytics, email us with the install details and we will pass on your request, or contact them directly through the links above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Our legal bases are consent (personalised ads, tracking) and legitimate interest (non-personalised ads that fund the game, analytics to keep it working).

Retention and transfers

Partners keep data under their own policies (GameAnalytics keeps raw events for a limited period, then only aggregates). Partners may process data outside your country, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help remove the data.
